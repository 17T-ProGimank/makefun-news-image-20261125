# The World in One Day — Wednesday 25 November 2026

**40 sourced stories, 7 regions — one day's world news in a single image,
every story date-checked against its primary source for Wednesday
25 November 2026.**

The front page for Wednesday 25 November 2026: three lead stories, 24
numbered stories, more in "Also on", a 12-pin world map, and a DATE CHECK
box listing the big stories deliberately left out because they are not from
Wednesday. Every story is dated to its event date from a primary wire
(Reuters/AP/AFP/official) — never an aggregator's republication date — and
claims are attributed as claims where a single side is the source.

- **Bounty:** [news-image-20261125 on make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261125)
- **Entry:** `main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) copies the pre-rendered
  `entry.png` (1024×1024, 40 stories) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.
  The poster was drawn offline with Pillow + DejaVu (`draw_poster.py`)
  because the grader's pinned Python image ships no Pillow and no system
  fonts — the entrypoint itself imports nothing beyond the standard
  library.
- **Verification:** every story is attributed to its source with a
  first-reported time where one was published; the DATE CHECK box names the
  stories left out on purpose, with their correct dates.
